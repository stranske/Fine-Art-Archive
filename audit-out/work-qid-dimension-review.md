## Method notes (deviations from the brief, and why)

1. **Counts differ from the brief.** The corpus has **834** sidecars with no
   valid `stable_identifiers.wikidata_q` (not 807) and **575** of those carry
   both dimensions and an artist Q-ID (not 566; it was 573 before the D020
   merge in note 9). The 265-artist figure is exact. All 834 hold a literal
   `null` rather than a malformed string.

2. **Dimension units are normalised.** The brief's `wdt:P2048`/`wdt:P2049`
   returns a bare number and discards the unit. Wikidata records artwork
   dimensions in mm, inches and metres as well as cm -- Monet alone has 57 mm,
   11 inch and 5 metre entries out of 1,094. This pass reads `psv:` and
   converts to cm, so those rows compare correctly instead of silently
   missing or falsely matching.

3. **Pagination confirmed necessary.** van Gogh returns 1,002 dimensioned
   works and Monet 1,094, so a single unpaginated page would hide roughly half
   of each. Every artist was paged to exhaustion; all 265 returned `ok`.

4. **The P18 check is crop-tolerant, and it was calibrated rather than
   assumed.** Archive masters are frequently 16:9 / 9:16 display crops, so a
   whole-image comparison against Commons scores near zero on correct
   matches. Measured on 84 ground-truth pairs (works whose Q-ID is already
   recorded) plus 84 deliberate mismatches: whole-image scoring gave 43%
   recall at `corr>=0.70`; sliding a master-aspect window across the Commons
   image raised that to **85% recall at 0/84 false accepts**. The threshold
   `corr>=0.70` comes from that curve.

5. **A failed image check is not proof of a wrong match.** About 15% of
   known-correct pairs still fail it because of crop geometry. Tier B is
   therefore reported, not deleted -- but it is not recommended for
   application.

6. **Dimensions alone are not sufficient**, which is why Tier A is the
   headline number. Several candidates agreed on both axes to within 0.1 cm
   and were still plainly different works -- `4465e11-andrew-jackson-sully`
   matched *William Gwynn* at 59.0x49.5 vs 59.1x49.5, and
   `9c8c00c-christ-on-the-way-to-calvary-rubens` matched *Portrait of Baron
   Henri de Vicq* at 73.9x56.0 vs 74.0x56.0. The image gate caught both.
   `title_sim` is reported alongside every proposal for the same reason; it
   is never used to accept a match, because it wrongly rejects correct
   cross-language pairs (`the-shellfisherman-toorop` -> *De schelpenvisser*,
   image correlation 1.00, title_sim 0.00).

7. **P31 gate widened after it over-rejected.** Chaining `P279*` to Q838948
   alone rejected four legitimate matches, because Wikidata does not route
   fresco (-> wall painting), photograph (-> two-dimensional visual artwork)
   or woodcut print (-> engraving) through it. The gate now tests several
   artwork roots. Q5 (human) and Q11424 (film) remain hard rejects; no
   candidate in this run hit either.

8. **The corpus changed underneath this run.** Something else is writing
   sidecars concurrently: between the scan and the report, four identity
   fields changed on disk (one work gained a Q-ID, one lost one, one gained
   dimensions, one gained an artist Q-ID). Tier A was re-validated against
   live disk state at write time -- 0 proposals superseded, 0 Q-ID collisions
   -- but this file is a point-in-time snapshot and should be re-checked
   against `work_qid_uniqueness` at apply time, not trusted blind.

9. **Run against the post-D020 single tree.** The corpus was re-scanned from
   `Art/works` after D020 collapsed the two sidecar trees and quarantined
   `Claude Project/staging_sidecars`. The merge moved little that matters
   here: 3 artist Q-IDs, 2 work Q-IDs and 2 dimension fields changed, and the
   eligible target set went 573 -> 575.

10. **Uniqueness is now enforced across proposals, not just against
    incumbents.** The first pass checked only sidecars that already held a
    Q-ID, which is precisely the defect `work_qid_uniqueness` documents (the
    by-creator backfill bound Q24046967 to three *Field with Poppies*
    sidecars because each search ran in isolation). Five pairs collided the
    same way here. **This corrects an earlier version of this file that
    listed 130 Tier A proposals: 10 of those were 5 contested pairs and are
    now withdrawn, leaving 120.**

11. **Both yield extensions are image-decided.** Tier A2 takes works where
    several of the artist's works sat within tolerance -- the image picks the
    winner, which must clear 0.70 *and* beat the runner-up by 0.10. Tier A3
    takes works whose h/w are transposed relative to Wikidata, where the
    image is mandatory rather than corroborating because a near-square work
    transposes onto itself (only 2 of the 28 are within 10% of square).
    Neither extension relaxes the +/-0.15 cm tolerance.

### Notes on the dimension defects

- **The brief's count of 11 is low; the target set holds 23.** Its two named
  examples are also misread:
  - Hokusai's *Great Wave* at 5.7x7.2 is **not** metres. x100 gives 570x720 cm,
    impossible for an oban woodblock print. Wikidata Q252485 gives 26.0x38.0 cm.
  - Riley's *Messengers* at 1000x2000 is **not** millimetres. Its medium is
    "Graphite and acrylic on plaster wall" -- a 10x20 m wall painting. Dividing
    by 10 would corrupt a correct record. No change recommended.
- **The largest single cause is a parser bug, not a unit slip.** For a raw
  string of the form `A x B x C cm` the stored pair is `(B, C)` -- it takes
  the width and the *depth* and drops the height. Corpus-wide this affects
  **14** works (8 in the target set, 6 in works that already carry a Q-ID and
  so were matched on wrong dimensions). Fixing the parser prevents recurrence;
  fixing the 14 sidecars is a separate backfill.
- The three *Ghent Altarpiece* sidecars resolve to the **Adoration of the Lamb
  panel** (133x236 cm), not the full polyptych (~350x461 cm). The brief's
  350x440 assumed the whole work. Which is right depends on what each master
  image actually shows -- worth an eyeball before applying.
- Seven works record an impossible height of exactly 5 cm against widths of
  33-202 cm. Six resolve against Wikidata; `e2d4e9f-flowers-in-a-wooden-vessel`
  does not and is left unresolved rather than guessed.
